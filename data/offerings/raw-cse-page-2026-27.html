CSE Tentative Undergraduate Course Offerings

Updated: July 8th, 2026

***For information on Summer 2026 course offerings, visit the Summer 2026 Preview of Classes.***

This page is tentative and subject to change. Course availability, instructors, and scheduling details are not final. Please refer to the following resources for official information:

  • Enrollment and Booking Calendar – Enrollment/booking dates and deadlines
  • Schedule of Classes – Number of sections offered, dates, time, etc. 

Instructor Listings:

  • If an instructor is listed as "Staff," it typically means a visiting lecturer will be hired. Lecturer names will be posted once the hire is official.
  • If no instructor is listed, the course will not be offered.
