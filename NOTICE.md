# Notices

- `lib/vendor/tritonplan/` — pure requirement-engine, academic-history parsing, program-matching and
  quarter-arithmetic modules copied from [TritonPlan](https://tritonplan.com), also by Sahir Sharma, and
  released here under this repository's MIT license. They have no DOM or network dependencies.
- `data/` — a snapshot of public UC San Diego information compiled for TritonPlan: General Catalog course
  entries and prerequisites (catalog.ucsd.edu), major and college general-education requirement files
  transcribed from the catalog and college pages, CAPE average-grade summaries from UC San Diego's public
  Class Planner endpoint, the Fall 2026 Schedule of Classes as published on tritonplan.com, the Enrollment
  and Registration Calendar (blink.ucsd.edu), and department tentative course-offering pages. Course
  descriptions and requirement text remain © The Regents of the University of California; they are
  reproduced here for educational, non-commercial use with source URLs on every record. No student data is
  included.
- Model inference runs on Nebius Token Factory using NVIDIA Nemotron models (NVIDIA Open Model License /
  OpenMDW-1.1). Web discovery and extraction use Tavily.
